**Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:WikiProject_AI_Cleanup  
**Shortcuts:** WP:AIC, WP:AICU, WP:AICLEAN  

---

## Overview

**WikiProject AI Cleanup** is a collaboration to combat the increasing problem of unsourced, poorly written AI-generated content on Wikipedia.

> "Wikipedia editors are making a guide to identifying AI-generated writing and the kinds of problems it tends to introduce. Your contributions are welcome!"

This is a WikiProject, an open group of Wikipedia editors. New participants are welcome.

## Key Resources

### Guide to Finding and Fixing AI-Generated Content

**[Wikipedia:Signs of AI writing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing)** is the primary field guide to help find and detect undisclosed AI-generated content on Wikipedia, backed by real examples.

**Recommended reading priority:**
1. Two "undue emphasis" sections under Content
2. "Superficial analyses" section under Content  
3. "AI vocabulary" section under Language
4. First two "communication intended for the user" sections
5. "Markdown" section
6. Edit summaries section

These sections alone will equip you to find AI text with fairly high signal.

## Important Policies

### Speedy Deletion Criterion G15
The most important policy provision specifically about AI-generated content is the speedy deletion criterion **[WP:G15](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Speedy_deletion#G15)**:

> "Speedily delete pages that could only plausibly have been generated by large language models and can be assumed not to have undergone meaningful human review."

### Writing Articles with LLMs
The closest thing to an explicit "AI policy" page: **[Wikipedia:Writing articles with large language models](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Writing_articles_with_large_language_models)** ([WP:NEWLLM]):

> "Large language models should not be used to generate new Wikipedia articles from scratch."

## Research Findings

Research shows that:
- Heavy users of LLMs can correctly determine whether an article was generated by AI about **90%** of the time
- This means if you are an expert user of LLMs and you tag 10 pages as being AI-generated, you've probably falsely accused one editor
- People who don't use LLMs much do only slightly better than random chance (in both directions)

## How to Participate

1. **Add yourself as a participant** in the project
2. **Inquire** on the talk page
3. **Read the [Guide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:WikiProject_AI_Cleanup/Guide)** to learn how to spot AI
4. **Use the [Signs of AI writing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing)** field guide
5. **Report issues** on the Noticeboard
